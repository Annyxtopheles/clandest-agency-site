Our process

How a project works with us.

Step 1

1. We talk

You tell us what you want to build, who your audience is, and your timeline. We give you a clear, fixed quote with no hidden surprises.

Step 2

2. We design

We share design drafts directly with you in Figma. You tell us what you like and what needs changing, and we iterate until it feels right.

Step 3

3. We code

Once you approve the design, we write clean, lightweight code. We test every page across phones, laptops, and different browsers.

Step 4

4. We launch

We hook up your domain, deploy the website live, and hand over all files. You own 100% of your code, design assets, and domain.
